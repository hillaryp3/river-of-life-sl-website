Events

Join us for worship, fellowship, and special gatherings.

Regular Gatherings

These are the rhythms of our community. Mark your calendar — and your in-world calendar.

  • Sundays Worship Service — 1:00 PM (EST). Music, teaching, prayer, and fellowship in our campus.
  • Monthly Ministry Training Night — learn how to serve on our team and grow in your gifts.
  • 1st Sunday Fellowship Breakfast — a casual morning get-together before the service.

Upcoming Events

A look at what's coming up. For the full live calendar, check our Facebook page or Discord.

  • Next Sunday Worship Service — as always, 1:00 PM (EST).
  • To Be Announced Ministry Training Night — interested in serving? Bring your questions.
  • To Be Announced Newcomer Welcome Social — brand new to Second Life? Come meet the team.

Kinds of Events We Host

🎵Worship Services

Our weekly gathering — worship, teaching, and community, all in one.

📚Bible Study & Teaching

Deeper dives into Scripture, book studies, and topical teaching.

☕Fellowship Gatherings

Casual times for conversation and friendship — the stuff that makes church feel like home.

🎉Holiday Celebrations

Seasonal gatherings with traditions, music, and shared stories.

✈️Outreach & Missions

Events focused on sharing our faith and supporting those in need.

🎤Special Services

Occasional celebrations for meaningful moments — dedications, anniversaries, and more.

How to Attend

  1. See what's coming up. Check this page, our Facebook, or our Discord announcements.
  2. Set a reminder. Add it to your calendar — or ask us to ping you.
  3. Teleport to campus. Use our Visit Us page, or search “River of Life” in Second Life.
  4. Come and be yourself. We'll take it from there.
