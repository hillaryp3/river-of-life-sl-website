Our Ministries

Ways we serve, grow, and connect as a community.

Every church is built on the hands and hearts of its people. Here are the ministry areas at River of Life — and every one of them needs you.

🎵Music Ministry

Music is a powerful way to worship God and connect with one another.

  • Leading worship during services
  • Singing contemporary songs and traditional hymns
  • Curating the service playlist

Serving: musicians, singers, and playlist lovers welcome — all skill levels.

🚪Hospitality / Ushers

Making every visitor feel welcomed and at home.

  • Greeting people as they arrive at campus
  • Helping newcomers orient themselves in Second Life
  • Keeping fellowship time warm and comfortable

Serving: no skills required — just a welcoming heart. We'll train you.

📷Photography

Capturing the moments that tell our story.

  • Photos during services and events
  • Documenting campus life and activities
  • Creating images for social media

Serving: camera-in-hand or just camera-curious, we'd love your help.

🎬Video / Media

Telling our story through moving pictures.

  • Recording services and events
  • Producing videos for social media
  • Maintaining our media archives

Serving: videographers, editors, and storytellers welcome.

🙏Prayer Ministry

Interceding for our church, our community, and the world.

  • Leading prayer during services
  • Caring for prayer requests (with care for privacy)
  • Hosting our in-world Prayer Room

Serving: if you love praying with and for others, this one's for you.

🎤Choir

Raising our voices together.

  • Singing during services and special events
  • Preparing arrangements for worship moments
  • Representing the church at special gatherings

Serving: vocalists of every level — the choir is as much about heart as range.

✨…and More, as We Grow

As our community grows, new ministries are emerging — outreach, small groups, and family programs are all on the horizon. If you have a gift and a question, talk to us.

How to Get Involved

  1. Tell us what interests you. Use our contact form or reach out on Discord.
  2. Meet the team. We'll introduce you to the people leading that ministry and answer your questions.
  3. Find your rhythm. Whether it's once a month or every week, we match your commitment to your life.
  4. Serve. That's it. We'll handle the rest.

We believe everyone has something to offer — whether that's musical talent, technical skills, a welcoming presence, a prayerful heart, or simply a willing hand.

No Experience? Fine.

Most of our teams learned as they went. What matters is the heart behind the serving.

Not Sure Where You Fit?

Come to a fellowship gathering first. We'll gently nudge you toward where you belong.

Ready to Serve?

There's a place for your gifts — and we'd love to find it together.
