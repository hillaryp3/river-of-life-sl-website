About River of Life

A genuine church community, gathered in the virtual world.

Our Mission

To help people discover Christ, grow in their faith, and connect with others who are seeking spiritual truth — all within the unique environment of Second Life.

Our Vision

We envision a world where technology serves as a bridge to authentic community, where people from anywhere on Earth can gather for worship and fellowship — without barriers of geography, disability, or circumstance.

What Makes Us Different

  • No physical location needed. We operate entirely in Second Life, meeting virtually for worship and ministry.
  • Inclusive by design. Our virtual campus welcomes everyone — regardless of physical ability, location, or schedule.
  • Biblical at our core. Despite our virtual setting, we're committed to sound biblical teaching and genuine Christian community.

Our Story

River of Life began as a small gathering in Second Life with a simple conviction: that faith doesn't require a building, and that technology can be used to spread the Gospel rather than distract from it. What started as a handful of believers has grown into a vibrant community that worships, serves, and grows together in the virtual world.

Our Beliefs

We believe in the core tenets of Christianity: the Trinity; the deity and humanity of Jesus Christ; salvation by grace through faith; and the authority of Scripture. We stand in the broader evangelical tradition while embracing the opportunities that virtual worlds offer for ministry and community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

Not at all. We provide step-by-step guides and live support for anyone new to Second Life.

Is there a cost to join?

No. Second Life is free to use — you only need a computer and an internet connection.

Can I attend without creating an account?

You can browse our website anytime, but to teleport to our campus or join a service you'll need a free Second Life account (a few minutes to set up).

What if I've never used Second Life?

No problem! Our New to Second Life page walks you through everything, step by step.

Is this a “real” church?

Absolutely. We're a genuine Christian ministry that meets in a virtual world. Our faith, worship, and community are authentic — they're just not tied to a physical building.
