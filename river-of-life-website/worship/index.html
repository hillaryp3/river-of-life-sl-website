Worship With Us

Every Sunday, wherever you are in the world.

Our Sunday Service

When: Every Sunday at 1:00 PM (EST)

Where: River of Life Church campus in Second Life

What to Expect

  • Worship music — contemporary songs and traditional hymns, sung together.
  • Bible teaching — clear, verse-by-verse teaching that helps you grow.
  • Prayer — praying together for our church, our community, and the world.
  • Fellowship — time to connect with fellow believers after the service.

New to Second Life? Our getting-started guide will get you from zero to front-row seat in about fifteen minutes.

Service Time

Sundays, 1:00 PM (EST)

How to Join

  1. Create a free Second Life account
  2. Download the Second Life Viewer
  3. Teleport to our campus

Can't Make It Live?

We don't currently livestream — the virtual world offers an experience that video can't match. But check our Media page for recordings and highlights.

What Makes Our Worship Special

Worship is about connecting with God and with each other. Meeting in a beautiful virtual world adds something extra to that experience.

🎵Music & Song

Our music ministry blends contemporary worship with traditional hymns, creating a rich tapestry of sound that lifts our hearts to God.

📖Biblical Teaching

We take our time with Scripture — explaining the text, its context, and how it applies to real life, so you grow deeper in your faith.

🙏Prayer

We believe prayer is powerful. During service we pray for our church, our community, and the needs of the world.

🤝Real Community

We're not just a service — we're a family. After worship we linger, chat, and build friendships.

🌐A Virtual-World Setting

Visual, interactive, and global — meeting in Second Life opens doors that a brick-and-mortar building never could.

🕊️Open to All

Come as you are. Whether you've been a believer for decades or you're just curious, there's a place for you here.
