Prayer Ministry

We believe prayer is powerful. Bring what's on your heart — we'll carry it with you.

How It Works

  1. Share your request. Use the form below — tell us what you're praying about, and how you'd like us to handle it.
  2. We pray. Our prayer team and the wider church intercede on your behalf.
  3. We keep in touch. If you opt in, we'll let you know when there's an update — quietly, and only if you ask us to.

Types of Requests

📢General

Prayers for our church, our community, or the world. Shared as we pray together.

🔒Confidential

We'll pray for you behind the scenes — no details shared with the wider church.

👤Anonymous

No name, no avatar, no trace. Just your prayer, in our hands and in God's.

📝Named

With your permission, we can include your name in our shared prayer updates.

A note on privacy: whatever you choose, your request is handled with care and discretion. We only share what you explicitly say we may.

🏠 The Prayer Room

We keep a quiet, peaceful space on our campus — open any time. Come sit, pray, or simply rest. If you're in Second Life, teleport on over.

Our Prayer Heart
