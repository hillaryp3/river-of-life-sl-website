Visit Us in Second Life

Your journey to our virtual campus starts here — and it's easier than you think.

Getting There

You don't need to know your way around Second Life — just pick whichever option feels easiest.

🔗Direct Teleport (Easiest)

Click the button below and we'll send you straight to our campus. (In the live site, this will be a Second Life / SLURL link.)

If you're not logged in, it will ask you to sign in first.

🔍Search

Once you're in Second Life, use the in-world search bar and type “River of Life” — our campus will appear in the results.

📍Ask a Resident

Know someone who's already in? Ask them to share our location with you — one click on their end, and you can teleport.

📱QR Code

We post a scannable QR code on our Facebook page — point your phone at it from the mobile viewer and you're off.

What's on Campus

Here's a quick map of the places you'll find on our virtual grounds:

⛪ Main Chapel

Where Sunday services happen — seating, a screen for teaching, and plenty of quiet corners.

🕊️ Prayer Room

A calm, candlelit space open any time you want to sit with God.

☕ Fellowship Hall

Our “crown and lounge” — come before or after service to chat, hang out, and just be.

🌳 Gardens & Grounds

Open spaces for reflection, one-on-ones, and a bit of fresh (virtual) air.

Service Schedule

Sundays, 1:00 PM (EST)
Services run about an hour; stay as long as you like afterwards.

Never Been to Second Life?

No stress. Our New to Second Life guide walks you from zero to “see you Sunday” in about 15 minutes.

What If I Get Stuck?

Message us in-world, or jump on our Discord — someone will walk you through it.
